Questions

Is TravelTropia free?

Yes. There are no paid plans and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No – you can try everything without one and your trips stay in your browser. With an account your trips sync across devices and you can plan with friends.

How accurate are prices, weather and visa rules?

Daily costs are built from typical local prices (coffee, meals, hotels, transport and sights) per country, and exchange rates update daily. Weather comes from real measurements via Open-Meteo – a live forecast for the next two weeks, otherwise last year's records for your dates. Flight prices are estimates – the flight and hotel buttons open real search results with your dates filled in. Visa and safety information was checked in November 2026, but rules change, so always confirm on the official sites we link to.

Where is my data stored?

Accounts and trips are stored with Google Firebase. Only you and the friends you invite can see your trips, unless you turn on a public link. Read the privacy policy.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time from Settings – it removes your profile, trips and friends from the database.
