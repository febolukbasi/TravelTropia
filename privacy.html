Privacy policy

TravelTropia is run by Trop, Netherlands. Questions or requests: TravelTropiaGlobal@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Your account – e-mail address and name (or your Google name and picture if you sign in with Google).
  • Your profile – handle, avatar or photo, home country, passports you add, age if you enter it.
  • Your travel data – trips, notes, expenses, visited countries, wishlist, trivia scores and badges.
  • Things you share – trip chat messages, meet-ups you post or join, and friend connections.

Who can see it

  • Your trips: you and the people you add to a trip. If you switch on a public link, anyone with that link can view the trip (read-only).
  • Your public profile (name, handle, avatar, level, visited countries): signed-in TravelTropia users, so friends can find you.
  • Meet-ups and daily trivia scores: signed-in TravelTropia users.
  • Passports, age, notes and expenses are only visible inside trips you share.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it on our behalf. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. If you use TravelTropia without an account, everything stays in your own browser.

Other services the app talks to

To show maps, weather and exchange rates, your browser loads data directly from: OpenStreetMap (map tiles), Open-Meteo (weather and place search) and ExchangeRate-API (currency rates). They receive your IP address and the place or map area requested – never your account details. Flight and hotel buttons open Google Flights, Skyscanner, Booking.com or Airbnb in a new tab.

Why

Only to run the service you asked for: saving and syncing your trips, letting you plan with friends, and showing leaderboards. We don't sell data, show ads or use tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage is used to remember your session and settings.

Your rights

You can see and edit your data in the app, export a backup in Settings, and delete your account at any time (Settings → Delete account). If you're in the EU/EEA you also have the right to access, correct, port and erase your data and to complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Children

Accounts are for people aged 16 and over. Younger travellers can be added to a trip by an adult without having an account.
